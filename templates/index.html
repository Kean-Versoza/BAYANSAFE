A Philippine Smart Community Disaster Intelligence and Preparedness Response Game

Every Filipino faces the threat of natural disasters.
Typhoons, earthquakes, floods, volcanic eruptions—they're part of life here.
But what do you do when danger strikes?

BAYANSAFE is a game designed to teach you how to stay safe during disasters. Instead of boring textbooks, you'll face interactive scenarios that test your real-world disaster knowledge.
These aren't just questions—they're skills that could save your life.


How It Works:

  • Enter a disaster scenario
  • Choose the safest action from multiple options
  • Get instant feedback explaining choices
  • Learn the correct safety procedures
  • Earn points and track your progress

Play Across Multiple Disasters:

Volcanic Eruptions

  • Fire & Thermal hazards

Wild Fires

  • Rapidly spreading flames

Earthquakes

  • Landslides & terrain collapse
  • Tsunamis & tidal waves

Flash Floods

  • Evacuation & safety rules

Typhoons

  • Severe flooding response
